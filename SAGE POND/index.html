About Us

Building the Future of AI in Africa

SAGE POND is a Uganda-based, research-driven artificial intelligence company dedicated to building the foundation for accessible, responsible, and impactful AI. By combining cutting-edge research with practical innovation, we develop AI infrastructure, language technologies, and intelligent solutions that empower people, businesses, and communities.
