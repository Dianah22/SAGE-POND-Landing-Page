Why We Started Unveyl

When we started training our first language model, Unveyl, one challenge became impossible to ignore: There’s barely any usable data online in local African languages. No Luganda. No Runyankore. No Acholi. Almost nothing.

And without data, even the best models can’t understand us. So instead of waiting, we decided to build something new: an open-source, community platform where anyone can contribute.

How You Can Help
